Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data Sentinel handles, why, and what you can do about it. The controller is Kejani Garage, Nairobi, Kenya. We aim to handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Contact: alex@kejanigarage.com.

“Salted hash” means we run your IP through a one-way function with a secret value. We can tell whether two requests came from the same address, but we cannot read the address back from the hash.

Who else handles data

  • Cloudflare: hosting, our database and optional cookieless web analytics.
  • Resend: sends us notification emails about messages, waitlist signups and reports.
  • Anthropic: processes the structured findings used to write AI fix plans, under its API terms.
  • Google Analytics: only if you click “Allow” on the analytics banner. You can change your choice any time with “Cookie choices” in the footer.
  • Google Fonts and flagcdn.com: your browser loads fonts and country flag images from them, so they can see your IP address and browser details.
  • Cal.com and WhatsApp: only if you click through to book a call or chat. Their own policies apply.

Some of these providers process data outside Kenya.

Cookies and local storage

Sentinel stores two small items in your browser: your analytics choice, and a short-lived cache of the public stats so the page loads faster. Google Analytics cookies are set only if you allow them.

Why we are allowed to use your data

We rely on your consent (analytics, waitlist emails), on the need to respond to what you ask us (messages, reports), and on our legitimate interest in running and securing a free public tool (check records and rate limits).

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose. You can withdraw consent at any time, and unsubscribe from waitlist emails by replying “unsubscribe” or writing to us. We reply to requests as quickly as we can. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

Data about websites, not people

Scan records are about websites. If you own a site and want its records removed, or want it excluded from future checks, use the form on Abuse and removal.

Security and children

Access to our database is limited to the site owner. Sentinel is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. For significant changes affecting waitlist subscribers we will tell them by email.
